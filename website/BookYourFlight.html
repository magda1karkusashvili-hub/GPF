Your adventure starts here

Plan Your Adventure

Choose your preferred location and connect directly with the local operator to discuss available activities, dates, prices and arrangements.

How it works

  1. Choose your preferred location.
  2. Call the operator directly, or complete the details to prepare a WhatsApp request.
  3. Select your preferred date and number of participants.
  4. WhatsApp will open with your completed request. Press Send in the chat to deliver it.
  5. Confirm available activities, prices and arrangements directly with the operator.

Activities depend on the location, weather and operational availability. Sending a request does not confirm a booking. Your arrangements are confirmed only after agreement with the operator.

Connect with an operator

To call, simply choose a location. To send a WhatsApp request, complete the details below.

Include + and your country code. Do not use spaces, brackets or hyphens.

WhatsApp will open with your completed request and connect you with the relevant operator. Press Send in WhatsApp to deliver your request. Opening the chat does not send the request or confirm a booking.
