About Us

At the Georgian Paragliding Federation, we believe the future of our sport begins with the people who make it possible — pilots, instructors, clubs and everyone inspired by the sky.

We work to strengthen Georgia’s paragliding community by promoting safety, supporting pilot development and bringing people together through training, events and shared experience. With Georgia’s remarkable mountains as our backdrop, our ambition is to build a lasting culture of responsible flying — where knowledge is shared, skills grow and every generation inspires the next.

Our interests include flight experiences, pilot development, aviation events and building a strong paragliding community.

We bring together people who share a passion for flight, learning and Georgia’s landscapes.

Mission

Support the development of paragliding in Georgia through responsible flying, education and shared experience.

Vision

Promote Georgia’s paragliding opportunities while respecting its landscapes and local communities.

Safety

Consider weather, equipment, pilot readiness and local operating requirements when planning every flight.

Development

Encourage training opportunities and the growth of an informed flying community.
