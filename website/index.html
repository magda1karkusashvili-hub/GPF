Discover Georgia from a Different Perspective

Our team consists of experienced pilots who put your safety and comfort first. Before take-off, your pilot will explain the flight details, prepare the equipment and guide you through what to do during take-off and landing.

Our pilots know the local flying conditions well. Fly with them and enjoy views of Georgia’s mountains and valleys from above. No previous paragliding experience is needed.

About Us

Georgian Paragliding Federation is dedicated to advancing paragliding as a sport and an aerial experience across Georgia.

Our interests include flight experiences, pilot development, aviation events and a strong paragliding community.

Contact our team to learn about current opportunities, locations and flight planning.

Get in touch ↗

Mission

Support the development of paragliding in Georgia through responsible flying and shared experience.

Vision

Share Georgia’s potential for paragliding while respecting its landscapes and local communities.

Safety

Consider weather, equipment, pilot readiness and local requirements when planning every flight.

Development

Encourage learning, training opportunities and the growth of an informed flying community.
