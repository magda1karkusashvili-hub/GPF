Before you fly

Need to know.

Understand the risks. Know your responsibilities.
Prepare for your flight with safety in mind.

Your safety starts
before take-off.

Read the information below before participating. Ask questions, share relevant information with your operator and follow your pilot’s instructions throughout the experience.

01

Understanding the Risks

Paragliding is an activity that involves risks. Proper preparation, equipment and adherence to safety procedures reduce these risks but cannot completely eliminate the possibility of injury or other serious consequences. Before participating, make sure you understand these risks and make your decision voluntarily.

02

Making an Informed Decision

Before flying, familiarise yourself with the participation conditions and safety instructions. If anything is unclear, ask your pilot to explain. Do not begin the flight until you understand what you will need to do during take-off, flight and landing.

03

Your Responsibility

Participants are responsible for providing accurate information about their health, weight, age and any other relevant circumstances, paying attention during the safety briefing and following the pilot’s instructions. Withholding important information or disregarding instructions may put both the passenger and the pilot at risk.

04

Health and Readiness

Inform your operator in advance about any medical condition, pregnancy, injury, surgery, mobility limitation or medication that may affect your attention, balance or physical ability. If in doubt, consult a healthcare professional before participating and share the relevant information with your operator.

If you feel unwell, dizzy, extremely tired or anxious before your flight, tell your pilot immediately.

05

Alcohol and Other Substances

Do not participate in a flight while under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any other substance that impairs your reactions, attention or coordination.

06

Safety Briefing

Listen carefully to the safety briefing. During take-off and landing, act only as instructed by your pilot. If you do not understand an instruction or believe you may be unable to follow it, say so before take-off.

07

Equipment and Personal Belongings

Do not open, loosen or adjust the fastenings on your helmet, harness or any other equipment. If you feel discomfort or notice anything unusual, inform your pilot immediately. Use phones, cameras and other personal items only with the pilot’s agreement and after they have been securely attached.

08

Behaviour During the Flight

Do not touch the controls or equipment mechanisms unless instructed by your pilot. Avoid sudden movements and promptly tell your pilot about any discomfort, nausea or fear.

09

Weather and the Pilot’s Decision

Your pilot assesses whether the flight can take place based on the conditions. For safety reasons, a flight may be delayed, changed or cancelled. Do not pressure the pilot to fly if they consider the conditions unsuitable.

10

What to Wear

Wear comfortable, season-appropriate clothing and closed-toe footwear with a good grip. Mountain locations can be cold even in summer, so bring a warm layer. Avoid flip-flops and loose items that could fall during the flight.

11

Age and Weight Restrictions

Age and weight limits depend on the operator, equipment, location and flight conditions. Provide accurate details when booking and confirm your eligibility with the operator. For passengers under 18, check the parent or legal guardian consent requirements in advance.

12

Weather and Flight Conditions

Paragliding depends on suitable weather conditions. Wind, limited visibility, precipitation or other circumstances may cause a flight to be delayed, rescheduled or cancelled. The pilot makes the final decision on whether to fly. Before booking, review the operator’s cancellation and refund policy.

This page provides general information. Confirm the requirements and participation conditions for your chosen flight directly with your operator.
